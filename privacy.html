Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who this applies to

Clipbot is a personal tool operated by nosoyj_ ("the operator"). It has no public users, no sign-up, and does not collect information from visitors to this website.

2. What Clipbot stores

When the operator connects their own TikTok and Instagram accounts, the platforms issue access tokens to Clipbot. These tokens, along with basic information about the operator's own posts (such as post IDs and view, like and comment counts), are stored only on the operator's own computer. Clipbot also stores the operator's video and image files and a local database of which items were approved and posted.

3. What Clipbot does not do

Clipbot does not sell, share, rent or disclose any data to third parties. It does not collect data about other people, apart from public comment text shown to the operator in a private summary on the operator's own computer. It does not run advertising or analytics on this website.

4. How data is used

Data is used only to publish the operator's approved content to the operator's own accounts and to show the operator how those posts performed.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the operator's computer until the operator deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time through the TikTok and Instagram account settings (the list of connected or authorized apps), which immediately stops Clipbot from posting. To ask for any data to be deleted, contact the address below.

6. Third-party platforms

Data handled by TikTok and Instagram is governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows when it was last changed.
